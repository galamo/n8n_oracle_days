### **1. Classic Vanilla Cake**

**Ingredients:**

* 2 ½ cups all-purpose flour
* 2 ½ tsp baking powder
* ½ tsp salt
* 1 cup unsalted butter, softened
* 2 cups sugar
* 4 eggs
* 1 tbsp vanilla extract
* 1 cup whole milk

**Instructions:**

1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease two 9-inch round pans.
2. Mix flour, baking powder, and salt in a bowl.
3. Beat butter and sugar until fluffy. Add eggs one at a time, then vanilla.
4. Alternate adding flour mixture and milk.
5. Divide batter into pans, bake 25–30 min. Cool before frosting.

### **2. Chocolate Fudge Cake**

**Ingredients:**

* 1 ¾ cups all-purpose flour
* ¾ cup cocoa powder
* 2 cups sugar
* 1 ½ tsp baking soda
* 1 tsp baking powder
* ½ tsp salt
* 2 eggs
* 1 cup buttermilk
* ½ cup vegetable oil
* 1 cup hot coffee

**Instructions:**

1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C).
2. Mix dry ingredients, then add eggs, milk, and oil.
3. Stir in hot coffee slowly.
4. Bake in greased pans 30–35 min.

### **3. Red Velvet Cake**

**Ingredients:**

* 2 ½ cups all-purpose flour
* 1 ½ cups sugar
* 1 tsp baking soda
* 1 tsp cocoa powder
* 1 tsp salt
* 2 eggs
* 1 ½ cups vegetable oil
* 1 cup buttermilk
* 2 tbsp red food coloring
* 1 tsp vanilla extract
* 1 tsp white vinegar

**Instructions:**

1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Mix dry ingredients.
2. Add eggs, oil, buttermilk, coloring, vanilla, and vinegar.
3. Bake 25–30 min in greased pans. Frost with cream cheese frosting.

### **4. Lemon Drizzle Cake**

**Ingredients:**

* 1 ½ cups all-purpose flour
* 1 tsp baking powder
* ½ tsp salt
* ½ cup butter, softened
* 1 cup sugar
* 2 eggs
* Zest of 2 lemons
* ½ cup milk
* Juice of 1 lemon + 2 tbsp sugar (for drizzle)

**Instructions:**

1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C).
2. Beat butter and sugar, then eggs and lemon zest.
3. Fold in flour and milk. Bake 35–40 min.
4. Pour lemon-sugar mixture over hot cake.

### **5. Carrot Cake**

**Ingredients:**

* 2 cups all-purpose flour
* 2 tsp baking powder
* 1 ½ tsp baking soda
* 1 tsp salt
* 2 tsp cinnamon
* 1 ½ cups sugar
* 1 cup vegetable oil
* 4 eggs
* 3 cups grated carrots
* 1 cup chopped walnuts (optional)

**Instructions:**

1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Mix dry ingredients.
2. Beat oil and sugar, then eggs. Add dry mix. Fold in carrots and nuts.
3. Bake 45–50 min in greased pan. Frost with cream cheese frosting.

### **6. Coffee Walnut Cake**

**Ingredients:**

* 1 ¾ cups flour
* 1 ½ tsp baking powder
* ½ tsp salt
* ¾ cup butter
* 1 cup sugar
* 2 eggs
* 1 tsp vanilla extract
* 2 tbsp instant coffee dissolved in 2 tbsp hot water
* ½ cup chopped walnuts

**Instructions:**

1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Mix dry ingredients.
2. Beat butter and sugar, add eggs, vanilla, coffee.
3. Fold in flour and walnuts. Bake 30–35 min.

### **7. Pineapple Upside-Down Cake**

**Ingredients:**

* ½ cup butter
* 1 cup brown sugar
* 1 can pineapple slices
* Maraschino cherries
* 1 ½ cups flour
* 1 cup sugar
* 1 ½ tsp baking powder
* ¼ tsp salt
* ½ cup milk
* 2 eggs
* ¼ cup vegetable oil

**Instructions:**

1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Melt butter with brown sugar in pan. Arrange pineapple and cherries.
2. Mix dry ingredients, then wet ingredients. Pour over pineapple.
3. Bake 35–40 min, invert to serve.

* 1 cup sugar

**Instructions:**

### **10. Strawberry Shortcake**

**Ingredients:**

* 2 cups all-purpose flour
* 2 tsp baking powder
* ½ tsp salt
* ½ cup butter
* 1 cup sugar
* 1 tsp vanilla
* ¾ cup milk
* 2 cups sliced strawberries + 2 tbsp sugar
* Whipped cream

**Instructions:**

1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Mix dry ingredients.
2. Cut in butter, then add sugar, vanilla, milk.
3. Bake 25–30 min. Layer with macerated strawberries and whipped cream.
